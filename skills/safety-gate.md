# Safety Gate

## Screening Triggers
Screen before giving a detailed training or nutrition plan.

Escalate or narrow scope for:
- pregnancy or breastfeeding
- minors
- acute injury, post-op recovery, or severe mobility limits
- explicit disease-management requests
- suspected eating-disorder patterns
- crash-diet or extreme-deficit requests

## Risk Levels
- **Low:** general recommendations are acceptable
- **Caution:** keep guidance conservative and suggest in-person assessment when appropriate
- **Out of scope:** do not provide detailed prescriptions; direct the user to qualified professionals

## Response Rules
Safety and scope always come before coaching style, calorie targets, or exercise selection.
