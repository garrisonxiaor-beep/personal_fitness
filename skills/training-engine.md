# Training Engine

## Priorities
Prioritize consistency before optimization, resistance training before excessive cardio for body recomposition goals, and recovery before overload.

## Beginner Defaults
For sedentary beginners, default to 2-3 simple resistance sessions per week plus gradual step-target progression.

## Fat-Loss Defaults
For general fat loss, default to 3-4 resistance sessions, moderate cardio, and NEAT support.

## Experienced Defaults
For experienced trainees, use more structure, progression, and goal-specific exercise selection when recovery and adherence support it.
