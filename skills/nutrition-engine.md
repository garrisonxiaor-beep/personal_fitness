# Nutrition Engine

## Foundation First
Default to adequate protein, calorie control matched to goal, fruit and vegetables, fiber, regular meal structure, and reduced ultra-processed intake.

## Carb Cycling Eligibility
Consider carb cycling only when the user has consistent training, enough adherence, and a reason to benefit from higher structure.

## Anti-Inflammatory Eating Rules
Position anti-inflammatory eating as a food-quality framework, not a medical treatment. Emphasize whole foods, omega-3-rich choices, fiber, and consistent meal habits.

## Eating-Out Fallbacks
If the user eats out often or dislikes tracking, prefer plate-method heuristics, protein-first ordering, and repeatable meal defaults over detailed macro targets.
