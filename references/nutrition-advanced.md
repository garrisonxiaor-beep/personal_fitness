# 营养进阶 Knowledge Base

Use this reference with `skills/nutrition-engine.md` when the user asks about detailed nutrition strategies beyond basic macro targets: carb cycling execution, peri-workout nutrition, diet breaks, refeed days, plateau nutrition troubleshooting, or eating-out strategies for specific training goals.

## 碳水周期深潜

### Scheduling carb cycling around training

| Training schedule | High-carb days | Moderate-carb days | Low-carb days |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 days/week full body | All 3 training days | — | 4 rest days |
| 4 days/week upper/lower | 2 heavy days (e.g., upper A + lower A) | 2 moderate days | 3 rest days |
| 5 days/week PPL rolling | 3 training days (push + legs hardest) | 2 lighter training days | 2 rest days |
| 6 days/week PPL | 3 heavy days | 3 moderate days | 1 rest day (lowest carbs) |

### Transitioning into carb cycling

1. First establish baseline: 2-4 weeks of consistent training + basic macro tracking
2. Then introduce high/low contrast: 2 high days + 2 low days, rest moderate
3. Adjust contrast based on performance and recovery feedback
4. Do not start with extreme swings (e.g., 8 g/kg high vs 0.5 g/kg low)

### When to exit carb cycling

- User cannot sustain the structure after 2-3 weeks of trying
- Performance drops on low-carb days despite adaptation period
- User develops anxiety around carb timing
- Simpler approach is achieving the same result

## 训练前后营养

### Before training (1-3 hours prior)

| Goal | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Hypertrophy | Mixed meal: protein 20-40 g + carbs 30-60 g + moderate fat |
| Powerlifting | Similar but emphasize carbs for CNS fuel |
| Fat loss | Smaller meal or just protein; training in a mild deficit is acceptable |
| Early morning training | Quick-digesting: banana + whey shake, or coffee + small carb source |

### During training

Usually not needed for sessions under 90 minutes. For longer sessions:
- Sip carb-electrolyte drink (30-60 g carbs/hour) for 2+ hour sessions
- Water and electrolytes sufficient for most training

### After training (within 2 hours)

| Goal | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Hypertrophy | Protein 25-40 g + carbs 30-60 g (whole food or shake) |
| Powerlifting | Protein + carbs to support recovery; prioritize if next day is also heavy |
| Fat loss | Protein 25-40 g; carbs optional if next meal is within 2-3 hours |
| Recomposition | Protein + moderate carbs if training was high-volume |

The "anabolic window" is broader than 30 minutes. Total daily intake matters more than exact timing.

## 饮食休息和再充碳日

### When to use a diet break

| Signal | Duration | How to execute |
|---|---|---|
| 4-8 weeks of continuous deficit | 7-14 days | Raise calories to estimated maintenance; keep protein high; reduce tracking stress |
| Performance crashing despite adequate protein | 7-10 days | Same as above; prioritize recovery foods |
| Hunger and food focus becoming overwhelming | 5-7 days | Smaller break at maintenance; then resume deficit |
| Psychological fatigue from tracking | 3-7 days | Eat intuitively at roughly maintenance; resume tracking after |

### Refeed days vs diet breaks

| Approach | What it is | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Refeed day | 1 day at maintenance or slightly above, high carbs | During prolonged cut when energy is low; 1x per 1-2 weeks |
| Diet break | 7-14 days at maintenance | After 4-8 weeks of deficit; when fatigue accumulates |

### Diet break rules
- Do not turn a diet break into an uncontrolled binge
- Keep protein at goal level
- Raise carbs primarily, moderate fats
- Expect 1-2 kg water/glycogen weight gain; this is not fat
- Resume deficit after the break, possibly at a slightly different level

## 减脂平台营养排查 Troubleshooting

### Step-by-step protocol

1. **Verify adherence**: Are you actually eating what you think? Check 3-7 days of honest logging.
2. **Check weekly average weight**: Use 7-day average, not single weigh-ins.
3. **Check waist and photos**: Weight may stall but waist keeps dropping → still progressing.
4. **Add activity first**: 1000-2000 more steps/day or 15-20 min walking.
5. **Add structured cardio**: 2-3 sessions of 20-30 min low-impact cardio.
6. **Small calorie cut**: Reduce 100-200 kcal from current intake (usually from carbs or fats, not protein).
7. **Diet break**: If steps 1-6 have been tried and fatigue is high, take a 7-14 day maintenance break.
8. **Resume at new level**: After break, start deficit at a level that accounts for any metabolic adaptation.

### Common mistakes

- Cutting protein to save calories (increases muscle-loss risk)
- Adding excessive cardio without adjusting recovery
- Panic-cutting 500+ kcal after one week of no change
- Ignoring sleep and stress as plateau contributors
- Using extreme low-carb or zero-carb as a "reset" (unnecessary and hard to sustain)

## 外食策略 by Training Goal

| Goal | Key strategy | Meal defaults |
|---|---|---|
| Fat loss | Protein first, ask for sauce on side, skip or share dessert | Grilled protein + veggies + small starch |
| Hypertrophy | Don't fear carbs; use restaurant portions to fuel | Protein + rice/noodles + vegetables |
| Recomposition | Moderate portions, protein emphasis, skip sugary drinks | Lean protein + moderate starch + vegetables |
| Powerlifting | Fuel for next session; don't under-eat before heavy days | Protein + substantial carbs + normal fat |

## 水合和电解质

### Daily water targets
- Baseline: 30-40 ml/kg bodyweight
- Training days: add 500-1000 ml per hour of training
- Hot environment: add 500-1000 ml

### Electrolyte context
- Sodium: important for high-sweat training; do not aggressively restrict during hard training blocks
- Potassium: emphasize food sources (bananas, potatoes, leafy greens)
- Magnesium: supplement only if dietary intake is low or cramps are frequent

## 进餐频率和时机

| Approach | Meals/day | Best for | Coach style match |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 meals | 3 | Simplicity, low-stress, beginners | 韩小四, 周六野 |
| 3 meals + 1-2 snacks | 4-5 | Most trainees, balanced MPS | Most coaches |
| 4-5 equal meals | 4-5 | Precise tracking, high protein targets | 凯圣王×谭指导, Pamela |
| Intermittent fasting | 2-3 | Preference-based, not superior for fat loss | User choice only; do not oversell |

Total daily intake matters more than meal frequency. Choose the pattern the user can sustain.

## 营养与睡眠

Poor sleep reduces:
- Insulin sensitivity (harder to handle carbs)
- Recovery capacity (need more protein and recovery nutrition)
- Willpower for dietary adherence

When sleep is poor:
- Keep protein at or above target
- Do not increase deficit
- Consider placing more carbs earlier in the day
- Avoid large late-night meals that might further disrupt sleep
- Prioritize sleep improvement over dietary optimization

## 安全提醒

- Do not recommend diets below 1200 kcal (female) or 1500 kcal (male) without medical supervision
- Do not recommend eliminating entire macronutrient groups
- Do not claim any diet cures disease
- If the user shows signs of disordered eating, stop nutrition advice and suggest professional help
- Do not frame cheat meals as "earned" or "deserved" — this reinforces guilt-based eating
